# Code of conduct

This project is for people who build speech datasets and the tools around them. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, is expected to keep it a place where that work is pleasant.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people who are still learning Urdu, Python, or both.
- Give specific, kind feedback, and take feedback the same way.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear instead of guessing at someone's motives.
- Respect the people whose voices are in the recordings. Only share audio and transcripts you have the right to share, and keep private recordings out of public issues.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's language, dialect, nationality, religion, gender, age, disability, or background.
- Publishing someone else's private information, including their voice recordings, without permission.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a request after a maintainer has answered it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. They apply the rules fairly and explain their decision when they can.

To report a problem, use the private reporting option on the repository's Security tab, or contact the maintainers through the XOVO Technologies organization on GitHub. Reports are handled privately.
